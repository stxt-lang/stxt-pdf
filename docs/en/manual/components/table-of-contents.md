# `Table of contents`

The automatic table of contents. A `Table of contents` generates an entry for each heading of the document, with its
title, a dot leader and the page where it is. Each entry is clickable and leads to its heading.

```
Document (dev.stxt.pdf):
    Language: en
    Title: Report
    Page:
        Paper: A4
        Margin: 50
    Styles:
        For element: Document
            Typography:
                Font: IBM Plex Sans
                Size: 10
                Numbered: true
        For element: Heading 1
            Typography:
                Size: 18
                Bold: true
            Box:
                Margin: 0 0 12 0
        For element: Heading 2
            Typography:
                Size: 12
                Bold: true
            Box:
                Margin: 10 0 6 0
        For element: TOC 1
            Typography:
                Bold: true
            Box:
                Margin: 6 0 2 0
        For element: TOC 2
            Box:
                Padding: 0 0 0 16
    Section:
        Heading 1:
            In table of contents: false
            Typography:
                Numbered: false
            Text: Contents
        Table of contents:
            Levels: 1 2
    Section:
        Heading 1:
            Text: Results
        Heading 2:
            Text: Sales
        Paragraph: Sales grew by 12%.
        Heading 2:
            Text: Costs
        Paragraph: Costs are holding steady.
        Heading 1:
            Text: Forecast
        Paragraph: A stable year is expected.
```

## Properties

| Property | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Levels` | `1 3` | The heading levels it lists, from the first to the last: `Levels: 1 2` lists the `Heading 1` and `Heading 2` elements |
| `Leader` | `dots` | What fills the gap between the title and the page: `dots` (dots), `line` (a line) or `none` |

It also accepts `Id`, `Style`, `Box`, `Typography` and `Break`. It has no children: the entries are generated by the engine.

## The entries

- **All** the headings of those levels appear, in order, including those after the table of contents and those of a
  `Markdown`. A heading with `In table of contents: false` does not appear (the title of the cover, that of the table of
  contents itself).
- Each entry has the text of the heading, with its number if it has one, the leader and the page number, the same one
  printed on that page.
- They are styled with `For element: TOC 1` to `TOC 6`, one rule per level: the indent of each level is its left
  `Padding`, and the spacing between entries, its `Margin`.
- If a title does not fit on one line, it is broken, and the number stays on the first line.
- A table of contents without any heading in its levels is an error.

More detail in the [reference](../../reference/components/table-of-contents.md).
